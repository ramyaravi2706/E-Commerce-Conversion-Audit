## 2. Project Overview

ShopEase is a mobile shopping application prototype designed to provide users with a simple and convenient online shopping experience.

The application allows users to browse products, view product details, select product options, add products to their cart, and proceed to checkout.

This project focuses on evaluating the user interface and identifying potential usability issues that may affect the shopping experience and purchase conversion process.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Evaluate the usability of the e-commerce application.
- Identify potential barriers in the shopping process.
- Analyze the product browsing and purchasing experience.
- Improve navigation and interface clarity.
- Provide recommendations to improve user engagement.
- Identify opportunities to reduce cart abandonment.
- Improve the overall conversion experience.

## 5. Application Screens

The ShopEase application consists of four main screens.

### 5.1 Home Screen

The Home Screen displays the available products, search option, product categories, and product images.

**Key Features:**
- Product browsing
- Search functionality
- Category navigation
- Product cards
- Bottom navigation bar

**Audit Observation:** Product information and labels could be more readable.

**Recommendation:** Improve text readability and maintain consistent spacing between product cards.

### 5.2 Product Details Screen

The Product Details Screen provides information about the selected product.

**Key Features:**
- Product image
- Product name and price
- Size selection
- Colour selection
- Add to Cart button

**Audit Observation:** Size and colour selection options could be more visually noticeable.

**Recommendation:** Use clear selection indicators and improve the visibility of product options.

### 5.3 My Cart Screen

The My Cart Screen displays the selected product and its price details.

**Key Features:**
- Selected product information
- Product price
- Shipping details
- Discount information
- Grand total
- Checkout button

**Audit Observation:** Quantity adjustment controls are not clearly visible in the current screen.

**Recommendation:** Provide quantity increase and decrease controls and make product removal options easy to identify.

### 5.4 Checkout Screen

The Checkout Screen allows users to review their order and proceed with payment.

**Key Features:**
- Delivery address
- Order summary
- Payment methods
- Price summary
- Place Order button

**Audit Observation:** Payment options and price details appear compact.

**Recommendation:** Improve spacing, payment option visibility, and readability of the final payable amount.

## 6. Conversion Funnel Analysis

The ShopEase shopping process follows these stages:

1. **Product Discovery:** Users browse products on the Home Screen.
2. **Product Evaluation:** Users view product details and select size and colour.
3. **Add to Cart:** Users add the selected product to the shopping cart.
4. **Cart Review:** Users review the product and total price.
5. **Checkout:** Users confirm their delivery address and payment method.
6. **Purchase Action:** Users select the Place Order button.

The conversion funnel helps identify areas where users may leave the shopping process before completing a purchase.

## 7. Usability Issues Identified

| Screen | Potential Issue | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Small product information text | Medium |
| Product Details | Selection indicators need better visibility | Medium |
| My Cart | Quantity controls are not clearly visible | High |
| Checkout | Payment options appear compact | Medium |

These observations are based on a visual review of the current prototype. Actual user behaviour and conversion rates were not measured.

## 8. Recommendations for Improvement

1. Improve font size and text contrast for better readability.
2. Highlight selected product sizes and colours.
3. Add quantity adjustment controls in the cart.
4. Provide clear product removal options.
5. Improve the layout of payment methods.
6. Highlight the final payable amount.
7. Maintain consistent spacing and alignment.
8. Make important action buttons easy to identify.
9. Provide clear feedback after important user actions.
10. Ensure smooth navigation between all shopping screens.

## 9. Prototype Interaction Testing

The prototype navigation was reviewed using Figma Present mode.

The main shopping flow includes:

- Home Screen → Product Details Screen
- Product Details Screen → My Cart Screen
- My Cart Screen → Checkout Screen

The Checkout button on the My Cart Screen is connected to the Checkout Screen.

## 10. Expected Benefits

The recommended improvements are expected to:

- Make product browsing easier.
- Improve the shopping experience.
- Reduce confusion during product selection.
- Make cart management more convenient.
- Improve checkout clarity.
- Encourage users to complete their purchases.

These are expected benefits; they have not been validated through live conversion data.
